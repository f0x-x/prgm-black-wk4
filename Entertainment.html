Antiquity and Early Stagecraft

Early forms relied on mechanical automatons (such as those by Hero of Alexandria), shadow play, and basic optics like the camera obscura and magic lantern.

Analogue audio and Mechanical reproduction

The late 19th and early 20th centuries introduced Edison's phonograph, early telephone-based broadcast experiments like the Théâtrophone, and projected moving images via the Kinetoscope and Cinématographe.
